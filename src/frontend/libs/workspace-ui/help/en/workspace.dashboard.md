Welcome to your workspace. This is your personal hub for accessing the tools and information relevant to your role.

## Apps

Use the app switcher in the sidebar to navigate between different modules:

- **Home** - your dashboard and profile
- Plus any workspace apps enabled for your organization

Each app you have access to appears in the switcher; selecting one opens its
dashboard and navigation.

## Getting Started

Your available apps depend on which modules are enabled for your organization and your assigned permissions. If you don't see an expected app, contact your administrator.

## Keyboard Shortcut

Press **?** at any time to open or close this help panel.
