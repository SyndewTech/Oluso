Choose how you want to be notified when automations send you messages. Each automation step you've previously received a notification for appears here so you can opt in or out per channel.

## Reading the List

Notifications are grouped by automation. Within each group, every step shows:

- **Step name** — the part of the automation that sent you a message
- **Last received** — the most recent time this step notified you
- **Email** — toggle to receive (or stop receiving) email for this step
- **SMS** — toggle to receive (or stop receiving) SMS for this step

Toggles save automatically — there's no separate **Save** button.

## When You'll See Entries

The list only contains steps that have actually notified you at least once. New automations appear here after you receive your first notification from them.

## Channel Availability

If your organisation hasn't configured an SMS provider, the SMS toggle is hidden. Email is always available.

## Mandatory Notifications

Some notifications can't be disabled — typically those tied to compliance, security alerts, or workflow steps where the admin has marked opt-out as unavailable. These appear in the list but the toggle is locked.

## Tips

- Turn off email for low-priority automations (e.g. weekly digests) and keep critical ones (approvals, leave decisions) on.
- If you stop receiving expected notifications, check this page — you may have inadvertently turned them off.
- Preferences are per-user, not per-device. Toggling here affects all email/SMS endpoints registered to your account.
