A personal file area only you can see. Use it to upload receipts, expense documents, signed forms, or anything else you want to keep with your account but not share with the rest of your organisation.

## Browsing

The page shows folders at the current level on top, followed by individual files. Click a folder to enter it. Use the breadcrumb at the top to navigate back up — clicking **All Files** returns to the root.

## Uploading

The upload area accepts:

- Images (PNG, JPG, etc.)
- PDFs
- Word and Excel documents (`.doc`, `.docx`, `.xls`, `.xlsx`)
- Plain CSV and TXT files

Drag files in or click to choose. Optionally type a folder path (e.g. `Receipts/2026`) to organise the upload. You can upload multiple files at once.

## File Actions

For each file the row shows:

- **Name** and content type
- **Size**
- **Uploaded** date

Click a file to download it. Use the **Delete** action to permanently remove it.

## Folders

Folders are virtual — you don't create them up front. They appear when you upload a file with a folder path, and disappear automatically when the last file in them is removed.

## Tips

- Files here are private to your user account, not shared with your manager or admins.
- For documents you want a specific module or your manager to see, upload them to the relevant module instead.
- Storage is subject to your account's quota — the file list shows total usage at the bottom of the page.
