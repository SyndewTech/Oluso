View your account information. This page shows your identity details as stored in the system.

## Profile Header

Your profile picture (or initials), display name, and email address.

## Account Information

- **Display Name** — your full display name
- **Full Name** — first and last name (if set)
- **Username** — your login identifier
- **Email** — your registered email address

## Roles

Your assigned roles determine what you can access. Roles are shown as badges. If you are a manager, a separate Manager badge is displayed.

## Need Changes?

Profile information is managed by your administrator. Contact your admin if you need to update your name, email, or other account details.
